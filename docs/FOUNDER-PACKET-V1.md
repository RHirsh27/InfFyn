# InfFyn founder packet: the version to validate

**Recommendation: build and validate one recurring audit product with two views of the same economic records.** The first distribution advantage is the fractional CFO's relationships. The product advantage to test is whether a customer can get a trustworthy, repeatable economic answer faster than assembling exports and spreadsheets themselves.

## What we sell

“Bring us a month of AI usage and business evidence. See the cost of serving a customer or completing an internal workflow, the contribution or outcome unit cost, and the assumptions that could change the answer.”

For customer products, that means included cost, reviewed revenue associations, customer/feature/cohort contribution and negative-margin investigations. For internal workflows, it means all included inference/tool/compute/review costs, accepted outcomes and separately modeled capacity value. Internal work is not represented as an unprofitable customer with zero revenue.

## Why the revision is better

The original PRD was strongest on deterministic math, tenancy, provenance, sensitivity and saved reports. Its weaknesses were conflating collections with revenue, reference pricing with actual cost, inference contribution with full gross profit, and Stripe connection with paid entitlement. The revised build addresses those distinctions and supports the internal-workflow use case without launching a second product.

The report card has three explicit parts: evidence coverage, economics basis and outcome evidence. There is no unearned letter grade, universal token-profit benchmark or automatic ROI claim. A missing price leaves a missing cost; an unsupported revenue association remains unallocated.

## Differentiator and limits

The claimed differentiator is a repeatable review process that preserves evidence, periods, methods and versions across cost, customer context and business outcomes. That is a product hypothesis, not a proven moat. A skilled team can reproduce a single report in SQL or a spreadsheet. InfFyn must win on repeated use, fewer mapping mistakes, traceability, speed to a reviewed answer and distribution through trusted finance relationships. Broader competitive research is retained in the earlier `research/` and `outputs/` folders; it does not establish customer demand.

## First customer and offer

Start with AI-enabled software companies and custom internal-agent teams reachable through the fractional CFO. They need source exports, a real recurring economics question and a finance/engineering reviewer. Several thousand dollars of monthly AI spend is a useful signal, not sufficient proof of a budget or a must-have problem.

Approved September 7 offer: **$349 USD per company per month**, recurring audits and executive reports. Private invitations give full complimentary access until revoked, without a card or automatic conversion to billing. Data connection is separate from payment. Stephen owns customer validation; engineering delivery is not conditional on proving demand first. Hosted activation still requires provider acceptance, accurate business details and functioning operations.

## Validation sequence

1. Demonstrate both synthetic audit types with the CFO; find confusing labels and missing decisions.
2. Onboard 3–5 consenting design partners after hosted data/security acceptance. Use one month of real records, reviewed together.
3. Capture the exact before/after decision, data repair time, report delivery effort and disagreements about attribution.
4. Seek at least two decisions changed and one paying customer who repeats or renews. A compliment, free upload or connected account is not paid validation.
5. Add integrations or monitoring only when repeated customer evidence justifies them.

## What would make us change course

- Buyers care about total spend but cannot name a recurring decision this improves.
- Most inputs require costly manual reconstruction or unstable allocations.
- Inference is financially immaterial and the wider cost picture is unavailable.
- The CFO provides all of the value while the software saves little time.
- Customers use one audit but will not repeat or pay the monthly price.
- Existing internal SQL/spreadsheet workflows are cheap, trusted and adequate.
